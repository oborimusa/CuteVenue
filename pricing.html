❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How does the commission work on the Free plan?

When a customer books your hall through CuteVenue, we take a 10% commission on the booking total. You receive the remaining 90% after the event.

Why do I get paid after the event?

We use an escrow system to protect both you and the customer. The customer pays upfront, but the money is held securely until the event is successfully delivered. This prevents disputes and ensures both parties are satisfied.

Can I upgrade from Free to Pro later?

Absolutely! You can upgrade at any time. Your hall will instantly become a featured listing with zero commission.

How do I get paid?

We integrate with Paystack and Flutterwave. Once the event is confirmed as successful, your payment is released directly to your bank account within 2-7 business days.

What happens if a customer cancels?

If a customer cancels within 48 hours of the event, you keep the deposit. If they cancel earlier, the deposit is refunded to the customer. This protects your time and availability.

Is there a contract or lock-in period?

No. You can cancel or downgrade anytime. We believe if we don't deliver value, you should be able to leave without any penalty.
